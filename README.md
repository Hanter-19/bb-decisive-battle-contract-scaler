# Battle Brothers: Decisive Battle Contract Scaler
A simple mod to adjust the difficulty scale of the Decisive Battle noble contract in Battle Brothers.

Use mod settings to adjust an additive multiplier to the Decisive Battle contract difficulty.

Can be added mid-save.

Can safely be removed mid-game too, but bear in mind that any Decisive Battle contracts that spawned while the mod is active will keep their modified difficulty! You can avoid this by setting the additive multiplier to 0 in mod settings and saving your game before removing the mod.
